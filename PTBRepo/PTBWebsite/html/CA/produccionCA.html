FACILITIES AND EQUIPMENT

Modern facilities: We have modern, functional facilities designed to optimize every stage of the manufacturing process. Their layout and production capacity enable us to provide agile, efficient service that meets the highest quality standards.

High-precision equipment: Our facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art technology, including CNC machining centers, EDM machines, and specialized equipment for mold fitting and finishing. This infrastructure enables us to manufacture high-precision components and ensure an excellent level of quality in every project.

On-site maintenance and adjustments: We carry out mold maintenance, repair, and adjustment at our own facilities, allowing us to reduce turnaround times, extend mold service life, and ensure optimal performance throughout the production cycle.

QUALITY AND CONTROL

Precision: We apply strict quality control procedures at all stages of the process to ensure maximum precision and compliance with each client's technical specifications.

Measuring equipment: We have high-precision metrology equipment and measurement systems that allow us to verify the dimensions and tolerances of every manufactured mold and component.

Certified quality: We operate under ISO-EN-UNE 9001 certification and utilize high-precision metrological control equipment—such as coordinate measuring machines (CMMs), fiber optics, and profile projectors—to ensure the quality of every part.
