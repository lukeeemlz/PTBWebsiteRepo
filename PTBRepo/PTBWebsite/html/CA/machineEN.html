Our infrastructure combines the technology and equipment needed to cover every stage of the production process. We operate a specialized workshop for mold manufacturing, maintenance, and repair—equipped with high-precision machinery—as well as a plastic injection plant featuring an extensive array of machines that enables us to manufacture parts of varying sizes and levels of complexity.

WORKSHOP MACHINERY

Our workshop is equipped with specialized machinery for the manufacturing, maintenance, repair, and adjustment of molds. Thanks to high-precision equipment, we carry out complex tasks while ensuring the highest quality and reliability.

FACTORY MACHINERY

Our production plant houses 15 injection molding machines with capacities ranging from 50 to 850 tons allowing us to manufacture parts of various sizes and levels of complexity with efficiency, precision, and high quality standards.
