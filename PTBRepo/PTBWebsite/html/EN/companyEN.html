ABOUT US

With a history spanning over four decades, our company has established itself as a benchmark in the industrial field, offering high-quality solutions grounded in experience, innovation and the trust of our clients.

We have two specialized plants and a technical office, enabling us to offer a comprehensive service across all project phases:

  • Plastic Injection molding plants, equipped with advanced-technology to ensure efficient processes and high-quality products.
  • Mold Manufacturing and maintenance workshop, specializing in designing, development and optimizing molds for various industrial applications.
  • Technical office, where we develop and manage every project, offering technical advice and customized solutions from the design phase through to manufacturing and production launch.

OUR VALUES

Our philosophy is built on three fundamental pillars;

  • Innovation, continuously incorporating new technologies into our processes.
  • Quality, ensuring quality in every product and project.
  • Commitment, working closely with our clients to offer tailored solutions to their needs.
Continuous improvement is a part of our identity. We constantly adapt to technological advancements and new markets to continuously offer increasingly efficient and competitive solutions, while always maintaining our focus on customer satisfaction.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Mold Development: We offer a thorough service for mold development, supporting our clients at every stage of the project. From design and engineering, to manufacturing, quality assurance and start of the production, we guarantee reliable and efficient solutions tailored to each application.

Technical Capability: We have a highly qualified team and machinery who enable us to manufacture highly complex molds with exact precision. Our commitment ensures stability, optimizing production times and the performance of each project.

Flexibility: Our extensive experience allows us to adapt to the demands of various industrial markets. Thanks to our technical expertise and flexible working methodology, we develop custom tailored solutions that fulfill the specific requirements and demands of each industry.

Experience: Our track record enabled us to collaborate with leading companies and top-tier clients, such as; Mercadona, Chupa Chups, Caprabo and Carrefour–developing plastic solutions tailored to their specific needs. This work proves our focus on superior standards, accurate execution, and trusted consistency.
